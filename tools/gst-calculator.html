GST Calculator India

Calculate GST amount and the final price quickly. Choose a GST rate and enter the amount.

How to use the GST calculator

Enter the amount, select the GST rate, and choose whether you want to add GST to a base amount or extract GST from a tax-inclusive amount.

GST calculation formula

GST amount when adding tax = Amount × GST rate ÷ 100. Final price = Amount + GST.

Common GST rates

This tool includes 5%, 12%, 18% and 28% as convenient calculator options. Confirm the applicable rate for your product or service before issuing an invoice.

Frequently asked questions

What is a GST calculator?

A GST calculator estimates the tax amount and total price from an entered amount and GST rate.

Can I remove GST from an inclusive amount?

Yes. Use the Remove GST option to calculate the pre-GST amount and GST component.
